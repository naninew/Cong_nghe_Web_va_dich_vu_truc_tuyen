ためらう

躊躇う

例: 彼は返事を躊躇った。

(Kare wa henji o tameratta.)

Revealed Answer

To hesitate; to waver

Godan verb (う), Transitive

To hold back from an action due to doubt, shyness, or uncertainty; vacillating before taking an important decision.

Example

質問に答える前に、彼は一瞬躊躇した。

He hesitated for a moment before answering the question.

psychology Memory Hook

Notice the foot radical 𧾷 on the left of both Kanji. Picture two hesitant feet shifting back and forth at a critical crossroad before taking a leap.
